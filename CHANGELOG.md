# Changelog

## v0.1.0-alpha2

Second alpha before the WoW: Forever launch. Not yet tested in the live client.

- Settings: one SempreUI page in the game's options with a switch per module and the few options modules offer; `/sempre optionen`, `/sempre modul <name> an|aus`.
- Mail overview: every letter at the mailbox sorted by days left, take all with one click (never pays cash on delivery), warning before mail expires on any character; `/sempre post`.
- Gold journal part 2: auction sales from invoice mail with cut and deposit, purchases per item at vendors and the auction house, profit per item with `/sempre gold items [ah]`, vendor check `/sempre gold pruefen`.
- Waypoints: `/way X Y [text]`, by map ID or zone name; the game's own waypoint marker, pins on the world map, Ctrl + right click adds a point.
- Minimap button collector: other addons' minimap buttons behind one SempreUI button; `/sempre knoepfe`.
- Fonts: Grenze, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono (SIL OFL 1.1) in SempreUI windows, optionally for the game's fonts; `/sempre schrift`.
- What's new window after updates; `/sempre neu`.

## v0.1.0-alpha1

First public alpha for WoW: Forever.

- Core: modules load on demand, `/sempre`, `/sempre perf` (memory per module), error collector `/sempre fehler`.
- Items: bags, bank, mail, equipped gear and gold of all characters; owner counts in the item tooltip; `/sempre items`.
- Gold journal: every money change by source, per session, day and week, gold per hour, vendor income per item; `/sempre gold`.
- Loot memory: drop rates from your own looting, value per kill, where an item drops, farm goals, value per kill in the mob tooltip; `/sempre loot`.
